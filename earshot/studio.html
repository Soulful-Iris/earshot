earshot

Put a song in. Take it apart.

What do you want out of it

Stems

This runs a source-separation model on a small machine with no graphics card, at roughly two to four times realtime — a three minute song takes about ten minutes. One job runs at a time and the rest queue, because two at once does not fit in the memory.

Anything the song does not contain comes back nearly silent rather than missing, so the level of each stem is shown and the empty ones are marked. A piano stem from a song with no piano is a real output and a useless one.

Files are deleted after __KEEP__ hours. There is also a dialogue meter for the other half of this project.
